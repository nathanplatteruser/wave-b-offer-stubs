1 · What it is

A COI / binder hygiene overlay — not an AMS.

BIND is a Wave B offer stub: a paper/ink seat (or toothpick pack) that sits beside the producer / CSR desk. It is presentation-complete for Zoom and booth — not a full SaaS, not Epic Policy Checking, not carrier binding.

2 · What it does

Overlay QA — AMS stays SoR

1. Paste binder / COI text

No AMS marketplace install. Browser intake of certificate / binder language and the request email. PDF binaries are refused.

2. Gap checklist

Heuristic rows: named insured match, AI / primary & noncontributory phrasing, WOS, notice days, limit vs request, description of ops.

3. Human still owns send

Producer / CSR / E&O counsel decide. BIND does not certify the COI, bind coverage, or mail the certificate.

4. Seat or toothpick

Monthly seat for the desk — or a one-shot toothpick pack while you test the wedge. Mailto hold on both.
